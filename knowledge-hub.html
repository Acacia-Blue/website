Knowledge Hub

Guidance organised around the job in front of you.

The Knowledge Hub is Acacia Blue's library of guidance, templates and training for community groups, campaigns, sitting members and third parties. It covers the financial and compliance tasks of a campaign, from establishment through to wind-down.

Request access What's inside

What's inside

Plain-English guidance, templates and training.

Content is organised by task rather than by legislation, so you can find the step you're on and see what it involves.

Guidance

  • Step-by-step articles for each task, from setting up a bank account and registering through to disclosure returns and winding up.
  • Tagged for four audiences: community groups, candidate campaigns, sitting members and third parties.
  • Separate libraries for federal and NSW, so you read the rules that apply to you.

Templates and training

  • Templates and checklists you can adapt for your own team.
  • Readiness guides and webinars ahead of key dates, including the federal rule changes on 1 January 2027 and the NSW election on 13 March 2027.
  • Referral to a named specialist when a question needs one.

How it's kept reliable

Checked against official sources.

  • Each article cites the official source it relies on, such as the AEC, the NSW Electoral Commission and the ATO.
  • Articles are reviewed by qualified legal and finance reviewers before they are published.
  • Articles are rechecked against official sources when figures or rules change.

General information only. The Knowledge Hub helps you understand and organise your obligations.
