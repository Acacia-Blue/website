Getting started

Strategy and implementation

Every engagement starts with a strategy session. We work through where your organisation is, where it's heading and what it needs to report, and recommend the approach that fits. Implementation then puts that approach in place, so your finance function works from the first transaction.

  • People. Clear roles, approvals and delegations, and training so everyone knows who does what.
  • Processes. Expense approvals, donation receipting, reconciliations and a compliance calendar, documented so they keep working when people change.
  • Tools. Xero set up for how you operate, your donation platform connected, and templates ready for the paperwork that recurs every cycle.

The ongoing retainer

Once you're set up, the retainer keeps your finance function current, well advised and accurate.

Knowledge Hub

Keeps your team up to date on the latest processes. Plain-language guidance, templates and training for every stage from establishment to wind-down, with referral to a named specialist whenever a question needs one.

Advisory

Ongoing advice on budgets, cashflow and board reporting, with electoral funding and disclosure requirements tracked against your calendar across federal and state jurisdictions, coordinated with qualified advisers where a formal position is needed.

Accounting Services

Accounts, reconciliations and reporting kept accurate and current. For some organisations we keep the books; for others we train your team to keep them and review the work with you.

Tailored to your organisation

The retainer looks different depending on who you are. We agree the right mix at the strategy session.

Campaigns and community groups

  • Knowledge Hub access for your whole team.
  • Advisory on budgets, cashflow and disclosure obligations.
  • Bookkeeping training, so your team keeps its own books with our review and support.

Third parties and significant third parties

  • Knowledge Hub access for your whole team.
  • Fractional CFO advisory, with board and funder reporting.
  • Bookkeeping included, with expenditure tracked by candidate and electorate.

Why we train campaigns

We'd rather leave a campaign able to run its own books than dependent on us.

Campaigns that understand their own numbers make better decisions and stay compliant long after polling day. Training your team builds capability that stays with your organisation, and it's how Acacia Blue can support more campaigns each election while giving long-running third-party clients the dedicated support they rely on.

Start with a strategy session.

Implementation is quoted once we've agreed the approach. Campaign and community group retainers are set by tier and transaction volume. Fractional CFO work for sitting members, third parties and not-for-profits is priced by role. Either way, you know the figures before work starts.
