What we do

Your compliance, accounting and reporting. One retainer, one team.

Knowledge Hub, Advisory and Accounting Services are delivered together for one monthly retainer, so nothing is left for a community group, campaign, sitting member or significant third party to piece together themselves.

Part of the retainer

Knowledge Hub

Practical guidance, templates, FAQs and training covering a campaign's whole lifecycle, from establishment through to wind-down. A single front door for questions, with a clear, coordinated referral to a finance, legal or compliance specialist whenever a question needs a named expert rather than a general answer.

  • Plain-language guidance for every stage of setting up and running a campaign.
  • Templates and checklists for the paperwork that recurs every cycle.
  • Coordinated referral to named specialists, not a generic directory.

Part of the retainer

Advisory

Compliance built into how an organisation actually operates day to day, not bolted on after the fact. Electoral law obligations, disclosure timing and the questions that come up along the way are handled through Acacia Blue's process, coordinated with qualified advisers who hold the underlying professional positions.

  • Disclosure and reporting obligations tracked against the campaign's actual calendar.
  • A clear line to a qualified adviser for anything that needs a formal position.
  • Guidance that stays plain and direct, even when the news is a deadline.

Part of the retainer

Accounting Services

The accounts, reconciliations and BAS kept current every month, not reconstructed under pressure once a lodgment is due, feeding into the cashflow and budget-versus-actual reporting your board or campaign team can actually use to see where things stand and decide what's next.

  • Accounts, reconciliations and BAS kept current through the month, not at the deadline.
  • Cashflow and budget-versus-actual reporting, so you can see where things stand.
  • One set of books, one contact, no reassembling records after the fact.
