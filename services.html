How we work

Built around what you need to run.

A campaign needs a finance function built quickly, then run by its own team. A sitting member or significant third party needs one that runs all year. We work differently for each.

For campaigns

We set up and train.

We'd rather leave a campaign able to run its own books than dependent on us. A campaign that understands its own numbers makes better decisions and stays on top of its obligations long after polling day.

01

Set up

We set up the accounts, systems and routines your campaign needs from its first transaction, connected to your campaign bank account and donation platform.

02

Train

We train your campaign team, ideally a volunteer with a bookkeeping or accounting background, to reconcile daily, track spending against budget and the expenditure cap, and prepare disclosures for the candidate to lodge.

03

Support

We review your systems regularly, check in weekly and answer questions between sessions, through to election day.

What stays with you. We focus on financial compliance and process. The candidate remains responsible for lodging disclosures, and questions about electoral law, such as who can donate, registration or what counts as electoral expenditure, go to the electoral commission or your own legal adviser. Your tax return stays with your own tax agent.

For sitting members and significant third parties

An ongoing finance function, run alongside you.

Your obligations run all year, not just at election time. We act as your fractional CFO and financial controller, so your finance function keeps pace with the work you're doing.

Sitting members

  • Public funding, office entitlements and ongoing disclosure tracked on one calendar.
  • Accounts and reconciliations maintained monthly, with BAS prepared as a registered BAS agent.
  • Cashflow and budget-versus-actual reporting you can make decisions on.

Significant third parties

  • Expenditure tracked by candidate and electorate, so spending can be measured against the caps that apply.
  • Registrations, returns and disclosures on one calendar, across federal and state jurisdictions.
  • Donor disclosure at volume, with donation platforms reconciled to Xero so your donor records are organised for reporting.
  • Fractional CFO oversight: budgets, cashflow forecasting, and board and funder reporting.
