What's included

We build your campaign's own capacity to manage its finances: set up once, trained properly, supported all the way to election day.

1Set up

Setup session

We work with the campaign to set up Xero, Hubdoc and Together Giving, integrated with the campaign CRM and connected to the campaign bank account.

  • Xero
  • Hubdoc
  • Together Giving
  • CRM

2Train

Campaign finance training

We train campaign team members (ideally a volunteer with a bookkeeping or accounting background) to reconcile transactions daily and run campaign finance reporting from Xero.

Budget and cap tracking

Daily reporting on actuals versus budget, and how spending tracks against the expenditure cap.

Disclosure process

How to match each donation in Together Giving to the bank statement and prepare disclosures for the candidate to lodge on time.

3Support

Systems oversight

Regular review of Xero, Hubdoc, Together Giving and the CRM.

Weekly check-in

A weekly call to review reports and budgets and work through questions.

Signal support

We answer questions on Signal as best we can between sessions.

What's not included

  • Software subscriptions
    The campaign pays for Xero, Hubdoc and Together Giving directly, separate to the Acacia Blue fee.
  • Lodging on the campaign's behalf
    We don't prepare or lodge disclosures. The candidate is legally responsible and submits every lodgement, and our training shows your team how to complete them.
  • Electoral law advice
    We focus on financial compliance and process, not electoral law questions such as donor eligibility, registration or what counts as electoral expenditure. Those go to the NSWEC helpdesk on 1300 022 011.
  • Public funding claim and annual disclosure
    Extra support with these is outside this service and can be quoted separately.

Setting you up for success

Before you start

A solid foundation

Start fundraising from day one, with books that are clean from the first transaction.

Every dollar you spend

Spending with confidence

Know where you stand against budget and the cap every day, so every dollar works hard in the final weeks.

  • Building and reporting on a campaign budget
  • Tracking spending against the expenditure cap

Every donation

Managing donations well

Keep every dollar you raise and give voters a donor list they can trust.

  • Reporting on donors not yet checked for eligibility
  • Ring-fencing those donations until they're cleared

  • Recording in-kind support, fundraising and loans
  • Reports for weekly and half-yearly disclosures
