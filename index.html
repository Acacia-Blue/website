Who we work with

Four groups, four different sets of obligations, each one covered in detail.

Community Groups

Community groups and values-aligned organisations building capability for the long term, not just one election cycle.

Independent Challenger Campaigns

Campaigns standing a candidate for the first time, setting up structures, accounts and disclosure obligations from a standing start.

Sitting Members

Elected independents with ongoing disclosure, reporting and office obligations that don't stop once the election is over.

Significant Third Parties

Third-party campaigners spending above the disclosure threshold, with expenditure caps that shift depending on the candidate or issue supported and need precise, ongoing tracking.

One retainer. Three pillars, one team.

Knowledge Hub, Advisory and Accounting Services come together for one monthly retainer, so there's one place responsible for closing the gap between an accountant, a lawyer and a spreadsheet, not three.

Knowledge Hub

Practical guidance, templates, FAQs and training covering a campaign's whole lifecycle, from establishment to wind-down. A single front door, with referral to a named specialist whenever a question needs one.

Advisory

Compliance built into how an organisation actually operates day to day: electoral law obligations across federal and every state jurisdiction, disclosure timing, and the questions that come up along the way, coordinated with qualified advisers.

Accounting Services

Reconciliations, BAS and the accounts kept current every month, turned into the cashflow and budget-versus-actual reporting you need to see where things stand and decide what's next.
