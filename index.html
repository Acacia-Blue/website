Our purpose

To be the financial backbone of Australia's community independents movement, so that compliance, disclosure and financial management are not what holds a campaign or a cause back.

Our mission

To support organisations that cannot justify, or do not want, an in-house finance team: setting campaigns up and training their teams to run their own books, and working alongside others as a fractional financial controller and CFO. Clear service boundaries, and one consistent point of contact per client.

Our vision

A more professionalised, better-resourced community independents movement, where campaigns and the organisations around them have access to the financial governance and compliance support they need.

Genuinely invested
